# About-page landscape photography

Selected September 4, 2026. These are real photographs of the Judith Basin region, not JBIS projects or evidence of an affiliation with the photographers/agencies. The supplied JBIS logo remains unchanged in its existing brand uses.

The About page provides linked source credits and the CC BY license. Local delivery files are WebP derivatives at 480, 960, and 1440 pixels wide (quality 82). They are resized without upscaling, have metadata removed, and are cropped by the responsive layout. No generative edits, compositing, recoloring, or third-party image embeds are used. Original photographs remain available at the sources below.
